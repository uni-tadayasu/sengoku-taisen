SENGOKU VS — Official fact sheet
Updated: 2026-11-28
Version: 5.13.0

SENGOKU VS is a Japanese-language browser strategy game by KASAI SOLUTIONS. Develop a castle town, recruit and organize officers, and choose how to expand your territory. Play a personal campaign against computer-controlled factions, or register a lord to join online battles, alliances, letters, and trading. Court rank now increases rice production, from no bonus at unranked to +2% at Junior Eighth Rank and +32% at Senior First Rank, without requiring a purchase. Basic play is free, with optional cosmetics, monthly support with a permanent cosmetic choice per paid period, and a paid 30-day officer reroll pass that affects officer acquisition. The game runs in desktop, smartphone, and tablet browsers; no app installation is needed. The game interface and detailed guides are in Japanese. The English introduction includes a starting guide.

Title: SENGOKU VS (戦国VS)
Developer and publisher: KASAI SOLUTIONS
Genre: Browser strategy game inspired by Japan’s Sengoku period
Language: Japanese game interface and detailed guides. English introduction and starting guide available.
Platforms: Desktop, smartphone, and tablet web browsers
Price model: Basic play is free. Optional purchases include cosmetics, a ¥780/month supporter plan with one permanent cosmetic choice per paid billing period, and a ¥480 non-renewing 30-day officer reroll pass. The reroll pass affects officer acquisition; jade itself is not sold.
Content: 256 officers, 129 castles and locations, and 64 facility types
Court-rank benefit: Rice production gains 0–32% across 17 ranks. The bonus is calculated before upkeep and surplus adjustment, follows the current rank, and requires no purchase.
Play modes: Personal campaigns against computer-controlled factions; online battles, alliances, letters, and trading after registering a lord.
