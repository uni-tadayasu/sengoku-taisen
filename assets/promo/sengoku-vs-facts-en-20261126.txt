SENGOKU VS (戦国VS) — Fact Sheet
Updated: November 26, 2026

SENGOKU VS is a Japanese-language browser strategy game by KASAI SOLUTIONS. Develop a castle town, recruit and organize officers, and choose how to expand your territory. Play a personal campaign against computer-controlled factions, or register a lord to join online battles, alliances, letters, and trading. Basic play is free, with optional cosmetic items and a monthly supporter badge. The game runs in desktop, smartphone, and tablet browsers; no app installation is needed. The game UI and official guides are currently in Japanese.

Title: SENGOKU VS (戦国VS)
Developer and publisher: KASAI SOLUTIONS
Genre: Browser strategy game inspired by Japan's Sengoku period
Platforms: Desktop, smartphone, and tablet web browsers. No app installation required.
Language: Japanese. The game UI and official guides are currently in Japanese.
Price model: Basic play is free. Optional cosmetics and a monthly supporter badge do not provide combat or resource advantages.
Content: 256 officers, 129 castles and locations, and 64 facility types.
Play modes: Personal campaigns against computer-controlled factions; online battles, alliances, letters, and trading after registering a lord.
Save transfer: Link the same lord across devices to synchronize progress. One device can control the game at a time.
Setting: Historical people and places inspire the game. Character abilities, faction placement, and progression use the game's own rules.
